System overview

Three models, one loop, every 15 s.

Hover a block.

Step 1 · Source localisation (2D)

Where is the leak, and how strong?

Input · readings of every detector over the last 30 s (sensor history), wind.

Output · probability of each floor cell being the source; release-rate quantiles.

TabPFN · classifies the source zone in context from 1,200 simulated leaks of the same plant. No gradient training.

Baselines · prior (uniform over pipes), concentration-weighted centroid, k-nearest neighbours on the same rows.

Step 2 · Hazard forecast (2D)

Where will the air be harmful in the next minute?

Input · per cell: down- and cross-wind distance to the estimated source, time, wind, release rate, mixing.

Output · P(toxic) and P(flammable) per cell, 0–120 s ahead.

TabPFN · regresses the concentration distribution in context; probabilities come from its predictive quantiles.

Baseline · forward physics: the advection–diffusion model run from the estimated source.

Step 3 · Crowd routing (2D)

A personal route for everyone, around the forecast.

Algorithm · CCRP: shortest paths on the floor graph with exit capacities, so people spread over doors instead of queueing in the cloud.

Use of TabPFN · not inside the search. Each step into a cell costs length + w · P(harm), with P(harm) from step 2. Replanned at every update.

Baselines · today's plan: shortest path to the nearest exit, gas ignored; the same planner fed by baseline models; perfect knowledge (upper bound).

Scaling to 3D

Same three steps in a 9 m hall: light gas rises, heavy gas sinks.

1 m voxels, machines and pipes with heights, detector masts on vertical pipes, time-stepped 3D dispersion, alarm from noisy detectors. The source model also returns the leak height. 9 plants, 4 never seen.

Generalisation

Few public gas-leak datasets exist. TabPFN still transfers.

We found four: a wind-tunnel plume around a 1:50 industrial site, a sensor grid with measured airflow, an office with HVAC, and a MOX wind tunnel. Each was used as test data; the models' context came from simulation or other runs.
